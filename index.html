Find dy/dx for 2x3 − 5y4 = 3y using the chain rule.

Read the worked solution
  1. Differentiate both sides with respect to x. Treat y as a function of x. 6x2− 20y3 dydx =3 dydx
  2. Collect and factor the derivative terms. 6x2= (3+20y3) dydx
  3. Divide by the coefficient of the derivative. dydx = 6x2 3+20y3
